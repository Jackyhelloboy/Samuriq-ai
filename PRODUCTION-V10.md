# Samuriq V10 · recorded delivery operations

Every assigned role accepts the current immutable policy version and its responsibilities before privileged work actions. Acceptance records retain role, policy version, name, timestamp and content hash; they do not grant roles. The owner publishes new versions and old receipts remain retained. Support remains available for disagreements. Registration records contributor consent and immediately balances a selected eligible project across active, signed TLs.

Hours are flexible within briefs, while deadlines and truthful daily reports are mandatory. Default daily cutoff is 23:59 IST unless a project sets an earlier time. TLs follow up each member, check evidence, publish useful feedback and report contact/no-response counts, blockers and next actions. Managers oversee delivery and documented access changes; the owner controls QC posting and payments.

Only client-QC-approved work is eligible under its accepted contract and explicit target conditions. Internal reviews/screenshots are evidence, not QC. Settlement is expected at month end on working days, Monday–Friday; weekend month end rolls to the next weekday. Delayed QC or settlement review may shift a cycle. This is not an unconditional date or automatic transfer promise. Approved past work is not automatically forfeited for a later reporting failure/removal. V9 direct-versus-vendor payroll routing and owner-private finance remain supported. Cashfree is not enabled without credentials and provider testing.

Public share buttons are removed. TL invitations use explicit codes, expire within 30 days, and admit 1–1000 contributors per link. Each person/pair member uses one slot. TLs can update limits, extend non-revoked links, or revoke them. Old unlimited codes no longer join. An atomic D1 batch couples slot usage to the inserted membership receipt, preventing oversubscription and duplicate slot use. Link quotas do not limit all direct website allocation to that team.

Managers may transfer contributors inside the same project; confirmed pairs move together, including repair of a split pair. Full-rate direct operational moves can complete immediately. Payer/vendor/financial changes require owner review, a prospective day no earlier than tomorrow, and fresh member acceptance. The owner sets the new assignment target and explicitly honors prior client-QC work pro rata at its old beneficiary and rate. Date-scoped history protects old work from subsequent assignment changes. Atomic roster guards prevent concurrent stale transfers/removals.

Handovers clear former helper grants, revoke portal launch tickets, reset folder verification, move non-private open support queries, and remove former TL current-member access. Drive viewer and Telegram permissions require manual handover. Project block/removal retains identity, banking, evidence and financial history, creates a private review thread and blocks captured work actions. Restore both removed pair members to the routing/review queue. Global account suspension remains separate; TLs cannot ban people or appoint roles.

The client work desk embeds the client only where the client permits framing. It cannot read cross-origin private activity, login, recordings or QC. No port-forwarding proxy bypasses client security. TL monitoring uses truthful portal launch events, daily reports, Drive evidence, TL review and client-provided QC. Last portal launch is labeled as an access event, not proof of client work. Team activity reads are scoped and indexed.

Real tutorial/Telegram links still need configuration, per the owner's previous choice. Work remains gated until ready. Platform acceptance and required tutorial/project checks precede community redirect. Drive video playback needs coordinator verification. No Telegram bot/channel creation or automatic external membership/QC verification is claimed.

Tests use disposable D1 fixtures and synthetic identities. Legacy suites explicitly pre-consent synthetic actors with test-only SQL triggers, never production migrations. New V10 tests exercise real role receipt flows, invitations, registration balance, transfers and finance history. Local concurrency is not a production-capacity guarantee. No shared production credentials or real transfers are created.
